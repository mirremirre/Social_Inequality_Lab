**Anya Elisabeth Benson**

This scoping review project aims to extensively map out the relationships between income inequality and various well-being constructs. Through a comprehensive search of peer-reviewed journals, this project seeks to identify key findings, examine the methodologies employed, as well as highlight relevant research gaps.
